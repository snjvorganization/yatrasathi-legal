Privacy Policy

YatraSathi — Your Journey Companion

Effective November 7, 2026

YatraSathi is made by Small Peak Studio. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your trips stay on your iPhone. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and we never receive your data.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Trips, budgets, expenses, itineraries, payment methods, notes, receipts, tickets and cover photos are stored only on your device.
  • Receipt text is read on your device (Apple Vision). Images are not uploaded anywhere by YatraSathi.
  • Settings such as your name, default currency and distance units are stored on your device.
  • Deleting a trip deletes its data. Deleting the app deletes all of its data from the device.

When the app uses the internet

YatraSathi works offline. It connects only for these features:

  • Exchange rates. To show live rates, the app requests rates from ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com). The request contains only a currency code (for example "USD"); no personal information is sent.
  • Places and maps. To place your stops on a map and measure distances, place names (for example "Denver, Colorado") are looked up with Apple Maps. Map images are loaded from Apple. Apple handles these requests under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Your location (only if you turn it on, see below) is converted to a town name with Apple Maps.

Location (optional)

Location is off until you allow it.

  • Show where I am uses your location while the app is open to show you on the itinerary map, find the nearest stop and fill in the place for an expense.
  • Share where we are uses your current location to write a check-in message. The message is sent only if you choose a recipient in the iOS share sheet.
  • Arrival reminders (if you turn them on and allow "Always") use iOS significant location changes to notice when you reach a new town and show a notification. Only the name of the last town is kept on the device, to avoid repeat reminders.

Your location is never sent to us and is never shared unless you send a check-in yourself. You can change location access at any time in iPhone Settings → YatraSathi → Location.

Camera and photos

The camera and photo library are used only when you choose to scan a receipt, attach a ticket or set a cover photo. Images you pick are stored on your device with the trip.

Sharing and exports

Check-in messages, PDF and CSV exports and backups are created only when you ask, and go only where you send them through the iOS share sheet (for example Messages, Mail or Files). Backup files contain your trips, receipts, tickets and photos; keep them somewhere safe.

Notifications

Arrival reminders are local notifications created on your device. No push service is used.

If you contact us

Report a problem, Send feedback and Request a feature open an email from your own email account to support@smallpeakstudio.com. It contains what you write, any screenshot you attach and, if you leave "Include app and device details" on, the app version, iOS version, iPhone model and language. Your trips are never included. We use these emails only to answer you and improve YatraSathi, and we don't share them.

Children

YatraSathi is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your choices

Because we do not collect your data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. You control everything on your device: edit or delete trips in the app, turn off location in iPhone Settings, or delete the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address and in the app, with a new effective date.
